A BASIC TROUBLESHOOTING REFERENCE

Diesel Starting Diagnostics

A truck that will not start can have several possible causes. The first step is identifying whether the engine will not crank or cranks without starting.

1. Engine Will Not Crank

You turn the key, but the starter does not turn the engine. Begin with these basic checks:

  • Batteries: Check the battery charge and look for loose or corroded connections.
  • Starting conditions: Confirm the transmission is in the required starting position and any required starting interlocks are satisfied.
  • Starting circuit: Possible causes include a fuse, relay, wiring, or starter problem. Further testing is needed to identify the fault.

2. Engine Cranks but Will Not Start

The starter turns the engine, but the engine does not begin running. Start by gathering more information:

  • Fuel level: Verify that the truck has fuel rather than relying only on assumptions.
  • Warning messages: Record dashboard warnings and any available diagnostic trouble codes.
  • Recent history: Note recent repairs, fuel filter changes, or symptoms before the failure. These details can help guide further diagnosis.

Use the Correct Service Information

These checks are starting points, not a diagnosis. Follow the manufacturer's service procedures for the specific truck and engine. Secure the vehicle before inspecting it, and never bypass starting interlocks.
